# Justification:
# 12V Input Protection Circuit
Diode used: 1N4007

The 1N4007 diode was selected because it has a high reverse voltage rating of 1000 V and a maximum forward current of 1 A, which is more than sufficient for a 12 V power input circuit. The diode is inexpensive and widely available, making it a practical choice for protection circuits.

In the simulation, the output voltage is approximately 11.3 V due to the forward voltage drop of the diode (about 0.7 V). When the supply polarity is correct, the diode conducts and allows current to flow to the load. If the supply polarity is reversed, the diode blocks current and protects the circuit.
